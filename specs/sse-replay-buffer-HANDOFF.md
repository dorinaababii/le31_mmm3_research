## Rollback path

- **Local revert**: `git revert <commit-sha>` then restart uvicorn.
  No data is persisted, so no migration rollback is needed.
- **Remote disable**: comment out the `ring.append()` call in
  `event_bus.publish()` and the `Last-Event-ID` reader in
  `cook_stream.py`. The SSE endpoint reverts to feature 23's
  pre-replay behaviour within one uvicorn reload.
- **Data retained**: none. The ring buffer is in-process and
  ephemeral.
